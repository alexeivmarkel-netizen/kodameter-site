Annual access

You can explore the sample meter before purchasing. Select Unlock live meter to see the annual price for your storefront. To restore an existing subscription, use Restore Purchases on the subscription screen with the same Apple Account. Manage renewal in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Taking a reading

Allow camera access in iOS Settings, aim at the scene, select a film and choose aperture or shutter priority. Hold freezes the reading. Try sample scene works without camera access.

Keeping whites and blacks

Place the center square over your subject. Use White or Black for a subject that fills the frame. To use a gray card, place it in the same light as your subject and set a reference in the exposure controls.

Film guidance

The film notes distinguish working estimates from published exposure recommendations. Open the reference for processing conditions and long-exposure guidance.
