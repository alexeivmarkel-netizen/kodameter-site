Privacy on iPhone

Kodameter privacy policy

Kodameter processes live camera frames on your iPhone to measure light and detect subjects. Frames are discarded after analysis. Kodameter does not save or transmit images, identify people, collect analytics or track activity.

Film choices and exposure preferences stay on your device. Delete the app to remove them. Revoke camera access at any time in iOS Settings. External film references open websites with their own privacy policies.
